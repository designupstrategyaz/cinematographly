# Demo job — acme, "Spring tune-up" (a finished sample, nothing generated)

This is what a job looks like after development and script, for the invented sample client `acme` (a bicycle repair
shop). Read it in order; nothing here cost anything and nothing can spend: the job is concept-only (budget 0) and has
no approvals, so the gate refuses every paid call.

1. `00-brief/BRIEF.md` — what was asked.
2. `00-brief/DEVELOPMENT.md` — the person's voice first, research, five concepts, two adversarial rounds, the pick.
3. `01-script/SCRIPT-spring-tuneup-v1.md` — the script with its scorecard.
4. `01-script/SHOTLIST.md` — the storyboard as a shot list (the next stage draws it).
5. `99-costs/ESTIMATE.md` — what the clips would cost, before anything is generated.

In a real job, each of these ends with your approval in your own words before the next stage starts.

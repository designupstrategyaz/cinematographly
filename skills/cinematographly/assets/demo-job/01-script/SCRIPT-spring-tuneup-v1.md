# Spring tune-up — commuters back on two wheels (sample)
Script v1 · 30 s · vertical-1080 · English · acme

## Control idea
A bike left all winter needs one booking, not a weekend of fixing.

## Dramatic question
Will the hallway bike ride again?

## SCRIPT

[HOOK 0:00–0:04]  Hallway, morning. A bike under a coat; the front tyre sags.
carried by: object

[SCENE 1 — 0:04–0:12]  The owner steps over the bike, keys in hand, and leaves for the bus.
OWNER (to herself): "Since November."

[SCENE 2 — 0:12–0:20]  Phone, booking page: two taps. Cut to the workshop bench: the chain gets oil.
TEXT: „Book online — back in 48 hours"

[SCENE 3 — 0:20–0:30]  Saturday. The owner rides out of the hallway door into the street.
OWNER: "Back on the road by the weekend."
END CARD: acme · Book online

# Security

**Report privately, not in an issue:** write to vladms@designupstrategyaz.xyz with "Cinematographly security"
in the subject.

In scope, most of all: any way a paid generation, an upload or a publish call gets past the spend gate
(`skills/cinematographly/scripts/gate.py` and `hooks/hooks.json`) without an open job, the approvals before it
or a linted prompt; a way to forge an approval (the quote check); a way the gate stops failing closed. Also:
anything that sends data somewhere the person did not ask for.

You will get an answer within 7 days. Please give us 30 days to fix an issue before you publish it.
